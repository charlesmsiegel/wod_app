About the names & sources

The Adept method assigns A = 1 through Z = 26, sums the values, and repeatedly sums the decimal digits to get 1–9. Divide by three and round to the nearest integer: 1 → 0; 2–4 → 1; 5–7 → 2; 8–9 → 3. The source's C0y0te example demonstrates zero. This tool applies ASCII case-insensitivity and ignores only spaces, hyphens and straight apostrophes; it invents no values for other digits or Unicode.

The four Essence descriptions here are short original paraphrases of Mage: The Ascension 20th Anniversary Edition, printed pp. 266–267 (PDF pp. 267–268). This handle procedure yields Primordial, Pattern, Dynamic or Questing.

Hermetic craft names are ordinary alternate names. The Revised Tradition Book describes a full Shadow Name formed by inserting the birth name between the first and remaining craft names, followed by honorifics. The True Name adds the Latin phrase and ten syllables that are not real words.

Our generator combines original consonant sounds, one vowel and a final consonant per slot, using the browser's cryptographic random source. Hyphens keep the ten defined syllables visible. The four groups are an interface convention. The result is fictional soundplay with no assigned meaning or Enochian translation; no check against every real-world language is possible. You choose the intended pronunciation of manual edits.

Everything calculates in this page. Entered names are not saved by this app or sent to an API. Copying is optional and uses your clipboard.

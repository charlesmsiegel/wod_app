Refresh discarded cards

Deep, satisfying rest (normally between stories) restores your discarded cards. Reverie or Rapture refreshes as many cards as Glamour gained; Ravaging does not. Active components stay held. Drawn Nightmares never return.

First-edition rules and scope
  1. Lay out one known Art and at least one appropriate Realm. Each Realm after the first costs a point of Glamour.
  2. Before drawing, decide whether to spend 1 Glamour for a second Bunk. Both requirements must be fulfilled; their successes add. A Bunk you cannot or will not fulfill makes the cantrip fail.
  3. After fulfilling the Bunk, describe your intended effect. The Storyteller decides its result within the successes available.
  4. Enchanted targets or a freehold use the Bunk successes. Mortal targets or witnesses require the Art’s Attribute + the Realm’s Ability against the highest relevant Banality. Successes cannot exceed the Bunk total. A failed roll gives temporary Banality; a botch gives permanent Banality. A changeling can invoke Banality to resist.
  5. Keep the cards on the table until the effect ends. Then discard one used card of your choice, or spend 1 Glamour to retain it. Retained Bunks are shuffled back into the deck.

Nightmares apply immediately when drawn, then leave the deck permanently. Follow their instructions with the Storyteller; this app never draws a replacement automatically. Recurring Nightmare’s additional taboo draw is handled at your table.

The optional advanced same-suit bonus and oathmate sharing are not automated. The Changeling Players Kit, p. 3, offers additional Bunk-selection and fixed-success variations; they are not enabled here. These cards retain the core book’s separate levels and successes.

Sources: Changeling: The Dreaming (1995), pp. 191–195 and 212–215, and the Cantrip Cards. Card descriptions here are brief paraphrases. Consult the book or printed text reverse for full power effects and success tables.

Artwork and replacement slots

Every face and text reverse has an explicit slot in catalogue.json. Your local images can be replaced and reloaded. Faithful scans, AI reconstructions and partial candidates are kept distinct; only confirmed faithful scans are eligible for the optional importer.

# Hermetic Houses: naming menu and source notes

The House field selects an affiliation independently of accomplishments and
other titles. It adds `, bani [House]` to the public craft name, and between
the arranged Shadow Name and other titles. The True Name contains that whole
Shadow Name. This follows the worked example in *Order of Hermes Revised*,
printed p. 69 / PDF p. 71. No House is selected automatically.

The catalogue contains **29 distinct named Houses and sub-Houses**, including
departed, absorbed and suppressed lineages. Aliases share an entry. The menu's
three groups describe the consulted Revised-era sources, not a claim that all
29 coexist, that every small House has full political status, or that later
M20 chronicles must adopt the same roster. Historical choices remain enabled.

The local PDFs were searched by House names and the relevant sections read.
The original page layouts for OR p. 35 and O97 p. 65 were rendered and inspected
to verify the two-column historical list and the damaged text extraction around
Castrovinci. Search results from the White Wolf Wiki served only to locate
possible omissions; each included House is supported by the local books above.
*Dark Ages: Mage* and *M20 Victorian Age* received targeted index searches as
cross-checks, not a complete reread. No separate *Ars Magica* or Chronicles of
Darkness roster was imported.

## Differences preserved rather than silently reconciled

- **Thig:** OR's timeline gives 1846–1999, while OR p. 61 explicitly places
  the Verditius merger in late 2002. OR p. 51 also describes the Xaos split.
  BT p. 21 depicts Thig as an active, large House. The menu notes all of these;
  it does not enforce a single end date.
- **Janissary:** BT p. 20's active, troubled House precedes OR's destruction
  and renunciation. Its historical option remains selectable for that era.
- **Quaesitor / Guernicus:** O97 p. 42 calls Guernicus the unused official
  name; OR p. 55 says the founder deliberately preferred Quaesitor to his own
  name. One entry outputs Quaesitor and records Guernicus as an alias.
- **Criamon and Jerbiton:** O97 p. 34 ties the former Great Houses' relegation
  to the Grand Convocation and gives Jerbiton a late-1400s date. OR p. 34
  places Criamon's absorption in the 1700s, and p. 35 places Jerbiton's near
  1300. Their dropdown notes flag the discrepancy without assigning a
  supposed reconciled date.
- **Shaea:** OR p. 35 gives 1412 admission into Ex Miscellanea; GT p. 100
  associates admission with the later Grand Convocation. Both agree on the
  smaller House's rise to major status in the 1980s. The catalogue does not
  turn Seshat or Crescent Moon into additional lineages.
- **Fortunae:** OR p. 35 labels the foundation 1910 but describes the early
  1900s and full status in 1936. The UI uses the shared, clear promotion date.

These are fictional WoD histories. Sourcebook descriptions of Chinese,
African, Egyptian, Islamic and European traditions are not claims of
historical or cultural authority.

## Implementation theory and review points

Theory: a House is an optional affiliation, independent of name arrangement
and earned titles. The existing pure composer adds that affiliation exactly
once; a static, cited catalogue supplies menu choices and context.

Reused: the existing composer, safe `textContent` rendering, normal static
build, and native `select`/`optgroup` controls. No backend or edition simulator.

Assumes: the supplied craft name and Shadow override exclude the House suffix
when a House is selected. Free-text titles are not parsed or rewritten.
Selecting an alias-bearing entry uses the primary House name in the output.

Watch: era labels summarize the consulted books, and Castrovinci's later
institutional survival remains unknown. Add any newly sourced House as a
catalogue entry with a citation; no composition changes should be needed.
